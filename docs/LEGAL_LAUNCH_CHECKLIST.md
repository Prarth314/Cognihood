# CogniHood legal launch checklist

The website contains detailed development-stage legal disclosures. They must not be represented as final legal approval. Complete this checklist with qualified counsel before accepting paying customers or marketing CogniHood as a commercial driver-safety product.

## Operator identity and contacts

- [ ] Confirm the contracting legal entity and any assumed name.
- [ ] Publish the legal entity name, physical/postal address, legal email, privacy email, support email, and accessibility contact.
- [ ] Identify the controller, any processor roles, data-protection officer, and EU/UK representative where required.
- [ ] Select governing law, venue, and an enforceable dispute process.

## Privacy and biometric processing

- [ ] Complete a data inventory and data-flow map for browser, Vercel, Supabase, MediaPipe assets, jsDelivr, and Gemini.
- [ ] Decide launch jurisdictions and obtain state/country-specific privacy and biometric review.
- [ ] Confirm whether face landmarks or geometry trigger biometric notice, consent, retention, or destruction rules.
- [ ] Adopt an exact retention schedule for accounts, trip summaries, consent, logs, backups, analytics, optional cloud frames, and legal holds.
- [ ] Implement private, verified access/correction/deletion/portability/appeal request channels and response deadlines.
- [ ] Complete a DPIA or similar risk assessment where required.
- [ ] Execute vendor DPAs, review subprocessors, and document international-transfer safeguards.
- [ ] Configure a paid/commercial Gemini plan appropriate for personal-data processing before enabling cloud analysis in production.

## Product, safety, and AI

- [ ] Complete independent camera-performance, demographic, accessibility, bias, false-positive, and false-negative testing.
- [ ] Complete human-factors, distraction, warning-comprehension, offline, thermal, restart, and long-duration testing.
- [ ] Confirm whether medical-device, automotive, product-safety, AI-transparency, workplace-monitoring, or insurance rules apply.
- [ ] Prohibit vehicle-control integration and high-impact decision uses contractually and technically.
- [ ] Establish safety incident intake, escalation, recall/disable, and regulator-notification procedures.

## Consumer and commercial terms

- [ ] Replace development terms with counsel-approved commercial terms for pricing, renewals, cancellation, refunds, taxes, warranties, liability, indemnity, support, SLAs, termination, and disputes.
- [ ] Add checkout and subscription disclosures before accepting payment.
- [ ] Verify age restrictions and any parental-consent obligations.
- [ ] Record legal-document versions and acceptance using a server-verifiable audit trail.

## Security and incidents

- [ ] Apply every production database migration and deploy required Edge Functions.
- [ ] Review and test RLS for anonymous and authenticated roles.
- [ ] Adopt vulnerability disclosure, incident response, breach notification, logging, backup, restoration, key rotation, and vendor incident procedures.
- [ ] Confirm custom SMTP, CAPTCHA, abuse controls, monitoring, and billing alerts.

## Intellectual property and accessibility

- [ ] Generate and review a software bill of materials and third-party notices.
- [ ] Confirm model, font, dependency, API, trademark, and dataset rights.
- [ ] Register and publish a DMCA designated agent if the service will seek U.S. safe-harbor protection for hosted user content.
- [ ] Complete an independent WCAG 2.2 AA audit and publish an accessible accommodation channel.
- [ ] Confirm the intended ownership and trademark strategy for the CogniHood name and branding.

## Documents requiring final counsel approval

- [ ] Privacy Notice and Notice at Collection
- [ ] Terms of Use and commercial subscription terms
- [ ] Safety Notice and AI Transparency Notice
- [ ] Acceptable Use Policy
- [ ] Cookies and Local Storage Notice
- [ ] Service Provider/Subprocessor Notice
- [ ] Copyright/DMCA Policy and trademark guidelines
- [ ] Accessibility Statement
