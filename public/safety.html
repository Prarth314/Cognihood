Safety

Safety and Product Limitations

Read this notice before using any monitoring feature. CogniHood cannot make driving safe and must never replace an attentive, unimpaired driver.

Effective: October 28, 2026 · Version: 2026-10-28

Never wait for CogniHood to tell you to stop. If you feel drowsy, impaired, distracted, unwell, or unsafe, pull over legally and safely. If there is an emergency, contact the appropriate local emergency service.

1. Not a safety system or driving authority

CogniHood is a development prototype that provides experimental attention-support indicators. It is not:

  • a medical device or diagnostic tool;
  • a fatigue, impairment, intoxication, or fitness-to-drive determination;
  • an emergency monitoring or dispatch service;
  • a navigation, collision-avoidance, driver-assistance, or autonomous-driving system;
  • a replacement for legally required vehicle equipment, supervision, training, rest, or professional advice; or
  • authorized to control steering, braking, acceleration, restraints, alarms, or any vehicle function.

2. No interaction while driving

Complete account setup, camera placement, permissions, calibration, session configuration, troubleshooting, and results review only while safely and legally parked. Do not touch, read, type into, reposition, or troubleshoot the device while driving. Mount devices only where lawful and where they do not obstruct sight lines, controls, airbags, or safe entry and exit.

3. Camera and algorithm limitations

Camera-derived results can be wrong or unavailable because of darkness, glare, shadows, eyewear, facial hair, camera angle, occlusion, motion, vibration, disability, injury, facial geometry, skin tone, device heat, browser scheduling, low memory, dropped frames, network delay, or software defects. A successful signal check does not guarantee later accuracy.

Eye-aspect estimates, PERCLOS, blink activity, head-pose variation, and low-engagement indexes are proxies. They do not establish what a person is looking at, thinking, feeling, or medically experiencing.

4. AI limitations

Optional Gemini output may be incorrect, inconsistent, delayed, unavailable, or based on incomplete visual context. AI output must be treated as supplemental and never as permission to continue driving. Cloud failure must not be interpreted as a safe result.

5. Warnings and accessibility

A user may miss visual or spoken warnings because of road noise, hearing or vision differences, browser restrictions, muted audio, device failure, disconnection, or cognitive load. Do not rely on any single warning channel. The absence of a warning does not mean conditions are safe.

6. Device, power, and heat

Use manufacturer-approved power supplies, mounts, cases, and cooling. Stop using hardware that overheats, swells, smells unusual, exposes wiring, blocks vehicle systems, or behaves unpredictably. CogniHood has not completed automotive-grade environmental, electromagnetic, vibration, crash, or long-duration validation.

7. Prototype and simulated information

Map, V2X, motion classification, comparison, coaching, distance, environmental, and reference features may be simulated, estimated, experimental, or incomplete. Labels should be reviewed before use. Prototype comparisons are not national rankings or validated population percentiles.

8. Prohibited high-impact use

Do not use CogniHood output to make medical, employment, insurance, credit, education, housing, law-enforcement, immigration, legal, disciplinary, or other decisions that significantly affect a person. Do not use it to monitor another person without informed authorization and a lawful basis.

9. Testing requirements before wider use

  • Independent performance testing across lighting, camera positions, devices, and diverse users.
  • Human-factors and distraction testing in controlled conditions.
  • Bias, accessibility, false-positive, and false-negative analysis.
  • Failure-mode, offline, thermal, restart, and long-duration testing.
  • Legal review of camera, biometric, workplace, insurance, and driving rules in each launch jurisdiction.

10. Reporting a safety concern

Stop using the affected feature. Preserve only non-sensitive diagnostic details and contact the maintainer privately through the method listed on the GitHub profile. Do not post face images, trip records, credentials, or personal data publicly.
