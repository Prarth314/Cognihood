Use rules

Acceptable Use Policy

Use CogniHood only for lawful, consensual, safety-conscious experimentation. These rules apply to the hosted service in addition to the Terms of Use.

Effective: October 28, 2026 · Version: 2026-10-28

You must not

Use CogniHood unsafely

  • Interact with, configure, or troubleshoot the application while driving.
  • Use an output as permission to drive or continue driving.
  • Connect the service to vehicle controls or defeat vehicle safety systems.
  • Mount hardware where it obstructs vision, controls, airbags, or lawful operation.

Use it for surveillance or identification

  • Monitor, record, analyze, or infer information about another person without informed authorization and a lawful basis.
  • Use camera data for face recognition, persistent identification, tracking, watchlists, public-space surveillance, or law-enforcement intelligence.
  • Build or enrich biometric, advertising, employment, insurance, or data-broker profiles.

Make high-impact decisions

  • Use outputs for healthcare, diagnosis, treatment, emergency response, employment, worker discipline, insurance, lending, credit, housing, education, legal services, immigration, or criminal-justice decisions.
  • Represent CogniHood as certified, medically validated, automotive-grade, or suitable for determining fitness to drive.

Harm the service or others

  • Upload malware, exploit vulnerabilities, overwhelm systems, bypass quotas, probe other accounts, or evade access controls.
  • Use stolen credentials, impersonate others, scrape personal information, or attempt unauthorized access.
  • Submit illegal, infringing, deceptive, threatening, harassing, discriminatory, sexually exploitative, or privacy-invasive content.
  • Attempt to expose server secrets, service-role keys, or data belonging to another user.

Misuse AI or third-party services

  • Bypass safety filters or use the Gemini integration contrary to Google’s terms and prohibited-use rules.
  • Send sensitive, confidential, regulated, or third-party data to cloud analysis without required authorization.
  • Use an unpaid or consumer AI plan for production personal-data processing where its terms do not permit the intended use.

Security research

Good-faith research must avoid personal data, service disruption, persistence, social engineering, extortion, and access beyond what is necessary to demonstrate an issue. Stop when sensitive information is encountered and request a private reporting channel through the maintainer’s GitHub profile. No safe-harbor promise is made until a formal vulnerability-disclosure policy is adopted.

Enforcement

The operator may limit, suspend, or terminate access; preserve evidence; remove content; notify affected parties; or report conduct when reasonably necessary to protect users, the service, or comply with law. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate and may be appealed once a private support channel is available.
