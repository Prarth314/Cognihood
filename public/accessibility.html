Accessibility

Accessibility Statement

CogniHood aims to provide an understandable and operable experience for a broad range of users, devices, and assistive technologies.

Effective: October 28, 2026 · Version: 2026-10-28

Our target

The development target is substantial conformance with WCAG 2.2 Level AA for the website and legal pages. This is a goal, not a certification. No independent accessibility audit has been completed.

Measures currently used

  • Semantic headings, labels, links, buttons, status messages, and landmarks.
  • Keyboard-operable controls and visible focus indicators.
  • Light, dark, system, and higher-contrast presentation options.
  • Responsive layouts and text that can reflow on smaller screens.
  • Reduced-motion support where the operating system requests it.
  • Text alternatives or accessible names for functional controls where implemented.
  • Visual and spoken warning channels in monitoring features.

Known limitations

  • Camera-based monitoring may not be usable for everyone and may perform differently across disabilities, assistive devices, appearance, posture, or movement.
  • Spoken alerts depend on browser speech support and may be unavailable or unclear.
  • Charts, dense monitoring screens, prototype maps, and real-time updates require additional screen-reader and magnification testing.
  • Color, focus order, zoom, error recovery, and mobile touch targets have not received an independent conformance review.
  • Third-party authentication emails and vendor interfaces are controlled by their providers.

Safety and accessibility

Accessibility features do not make interaction while driving safe. Configure assistive technology and review results only while safely parked. Never rely on a visual or spoken CogniHood alert as the only safety channel.

Feedback and accommodations

To report a barrier, include the page or feature, browser/device, assistive technology, expected behavior, and a non-sensitive description of what occurred. Contact the maintainer through the method listed on the GitHub profile and request a private channel if the report includes personal information. A dedicated accessibility contact and response-time commitment must be established before commercial launch.

Assessment status

Current status: self-evaluated during development; not independently audited; not certified. The statement will be updated after formal testing and material interface changes.
