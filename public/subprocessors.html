International processing

Providers and their subprocessors may process information in multiple countries. Data residency, transfer safeguards, provider subprocessors, retention, and breach-notification commitments must be contractually reviewed before launching in regions that impose additional requirements.

Provider changes

This page will be updated before a new provider receives personal information for a materially different purpose. A formal advance-notice and objection process for enterprise customers has not yet been adopted.
