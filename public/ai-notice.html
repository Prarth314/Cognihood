AI Transparency Notice

CogniHood combines local computer vision, deterministic calculations, and optional generative-AI assistance. This notice explains what each part does and what it cannot establish.

Effective: October 28, 2026 · Version: 2026-10-28

Local computer vision

MediaPipe Face Landmarker estimates facial landmarks from live browser-camera frames. CogniHood derives experimental measurements such as eye-aspect ratio, blink activity, PERCLOS, head-pose variation, and signal quality. Rolling calculations and a conservative state engine convert those signals into prototype warning states.

Optional Gemini assistance

When separately enabled for a session, periodically compressed frames may pass through an authenticated Supabase Edge Function to a configured Google Gemini model. The function requests structured observations, validates output shape and numeric ranges, applies timeouts and quotas, and combines the result with local signals. A failed cloud call is not treated as evidence of safety.

What the system does not do

  • It does not recognize identity or compare a face against an identity database.
  • It does not diagnose fatigue, impairment, illness, stress, or a medical condition.
  • It does not prove attention, intent, cognition, gaze target, or fitness to drive.
  • It does not make legal or similarly significant decisions about a person.
  • It does not control a vehicle or contact emergency services.

Human control

The user chooses whether to grant camera permission and whether to enable cloud enhancement for each session. The user can disable cloud analysis, stop a session, export records, and request account deletion. The driver remains responsible for driving decisions and must stop safely whenever conditions warrant.

Known risks

False positives, false negatives, delay, model hallucination, device differences, demographic performance differences, and failure under difficult lighting or pose are possible. Independent bias, accessibility, human-factors, and safety validation has not been completed. Do not use the service for high-impact decisions or claim validated performance.

Data and vendor terms

Local processing remains on the device except for downloaded model/runtime assets and normal service requests. Optional cloud processing is governed by the Privacy Notice and Service Providers Notice. Google’s handling differs by API plan; an appropriate commercial configuration must be verified before production use.
