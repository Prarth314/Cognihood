Operator and contact status

The current service is maintained through the Prarth314/Cognihood GitHub repository. A separate incorporated operator, postal address, dedicated legal email, privacy officer, and registered copyright agent have not yet been published.

Do not place passwords, API keys, face images, driving records, health information, or other sensitive data in a public GitHub issue. Use the in-product export and account-deletion controls for account data. For other requests, contact the maintainer through the contact method listed on the Prarth314 GitHub profile and request a private channel.

Priority launch blockers

  • Obtain review from privacy, product-liability, consumer-protection, biometric, accessibility, and open-source counsel.
  • Publish verified business and privacy contact details.
  • Adopt and technically enforce an exact retention schedule.
  • Complete age-gating, consent logging, regional rights workflows, and appeal handling.
  • Confirm the Gemini commercial data plan before sending any production camera frame.
  • Complete independent safety, accessibility, bias, and human-factors validation.
