Last updated: October 13, 2026. These are development-stage notices, not final commercial terms.

Not a driving determination

CogniHood provides experimental camera-derived attention indicators. It is not a medical device, emergency service, navigation system, vehicle-control system, or substitute for an attentive driver. Do not rely on it to decide whether someone is fit to drive.

Safe use

Do not interact with the screen while driving. Obey applicable laws and stop in a safe location before reviewing information or changing settings. If you feel tired, impaired, or distracted, stop safely regardless of what CogniHood displays.

Prototype features

Map, V2X, reference comparison, phone-motion, and coaching features may be simulated or estimated and are labeled accordingly.
