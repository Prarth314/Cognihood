CogniHood development privacy notice

Last updated: October 13, 2026. This notice describes the current development build and must be reviewed by qualified counsel before a public launch.

Data used

CogniHood processes camera-derived facial landmarks, attention indicators, session events, account email, and session summaries. Raw video is not stored by the application. When you explicitly enable cloud enhancement for a session, periodically compressed frames are sent through a Supabase Edge Function to Google Gemini.

Purpose and limitations

The data supports experimental attention indicators and coaching. It is not used to diagnose a condition or certify fitness to drive. Synthetic prototype comparisons are not population statistics.

Control

Cloud enhancement is optional per session. You can export session records from Trip archives or permanently delete the account from the home screen. Account deletion cascades through CogniHood's Supabase tables.

Before production

The operator must add its legal entity, contact address, supported jurisdictions, exact retention periods, subprocessors, lawful basis, and request/appeal process before launch.
